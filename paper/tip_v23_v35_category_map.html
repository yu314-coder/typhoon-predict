Typhoon Tip (1979): real track vs. v23 and v35 mean forecasts

Same three-panel layout and map extent so position and intensity-category error are both visible at a glance. The two model panels are each model's mean forecast by valid time (solid line, category-colored dots -- dashed stroke marks it as a forecast, not observed). Tip is the most intense tropical cyclone ever recorded; both models are known to systematically under-predict its peak intensity (see the mean-forecast-vs-real vmax chart from earlier this session) -- this map shows where that shows up as a wrong CATEGORY, not just a wrong number.
